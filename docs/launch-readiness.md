# RelationSync launch readiness

## Approved positioning
RelationSync is a facilitated-practice cohort for individual participants, whether single or partnered. Short teaching, structured practice and selected live coaching. It is not a couples treatment group, community membership, app, or an offsession coaching channel.

## Current build scope
Static marketing website on the existing repository, preserving warm palette and coach.jpg. Existing coaching-client proof is sourced from https://robertsawyer.co/#client-stories, not represented as cohort-specific evidence. Inquiry uses the owner's existing email address. An email-draft link does not capture leads, enroll someone, reserve a place, or send anything from the website.

## Before paid enrollment opens
- Approve cohort dates, timezone, duration, session frequency and price. These are deliberately not inferred from old Attachment Reset copy or competitor programs.
- Set participant capacity based on facilitated practice and supervision, not an invented scarcity number.
- Approve syllabus, participant workbook, exercise instructions and facilitator run-of-show. Website milestones do not prove these delivery materials are complete.
- Set screening and referral process. Individual responsibility does not mean accepting abuse or taking responsibility for another person's behavior.
- Approve participation, passing, confidentiality expectations, recording consent, data retention and deletion policies. Peer confidentiality cannot be guaranteed by the website.
- Approve attendance and cancellation terms. Do not promise private makeups for fixed cohort dates.
- Verify agreement acceptance, payment receipt, administrative email delivery and Zoom access end to end with authorized testing.
- Decide whether to use a real interest form; if so establish approved storage, consent, receipt and administrative ownership before replacing email inquiry. No automatic marketing subscription.
